    How to play

    1. Pick a jar, then tap another to pour between them. Tap the same jar again to put it down.
    2. Colours mix like paint, not like light: blue and yellow make green. Two units of crimson and two of yellow make four units of orange — and there is no way to separate them again. Only the proportions matter, never the order you pour in.
    3. The big jar must end up full and matching the target shade. Full but wrong does not count.
    4. The sink throws liquid away. Use it to undo an overpour — but it costs you moves.
    5. Par is the fewest moves possible. One unit poured is one move. Beat par for three stars.

    Not every jar is meant to be used, and not every jar is meant to be emptied.
